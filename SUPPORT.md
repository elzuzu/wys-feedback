# WYS support

Use the [bug report form](https://github.com/elzuzu/wys-feedback/issues/new?template=bug-report.yml) for unexpected behavior, or the [suggestion form](https://github.com/elzuzu/wys-feedback/issues/new?template=feature-request.yml) for improvements. Search existing reports first. English or French. For anything private: **wys@elzuzu.ch.eu.org**.

## A useful report

Include the WYS version (WYS → About WYS), your macOS version and Mac model, the transcription model and audio language chosen in Settings, whether the audio is an imported file, a microphone recording or a system-audio recording, and the exact steps. **Do not attach recordings or transcripts** (they can contain other people's voices): describe the content, or reproduce the problem with a synthetic sample.

## Transcription quality

No automatic transcription is perfect, especially with noise, strong accents or proper names. WYS keeps the original transcription and your corrections apart, and flags passages that may be incomplete. Settings → Transcription model offers three built-in models; WYS switches to another one when the chosen model does not cover the language or is not validated for it. Validated languages are measured on read speech: see the in-app help for the list and what "validated" means.

## Recording what your Mac plays

In File → New Recording…, choose **System Audio** or **Both**. The first time, macOS asks to allow WYS to record system audio (System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen & System Audio Recording). WYS records all the sound the Mac plays, whatever the app, only while you record, never the screen, and keeps the audio on your Mac. With **Both**, your voice and system audio go on two tracks: the transcript names your passages and tells the other voices apart. Headphones give the best separation. If the system audio is silent, check the permission above, then record again; microphone-only recording does not depend on the system audio permission. **Tell the people you record**: recording others may require their consent, depending on the country.

## Summaries

The **Summarize** button in the transcript header uses the language model built into macOS 26 or later (Apple Intelligence), which runs on your Mac. A language model can be wrong: check names, numbers and negations against the recording.

## Free version and purchase

WYS is free to download and includes 60 minutes of transcription. A one-time in-app purchase unlocks unlimited transcription; there is no subscription. Apple handles purchases, billing and refunds. The Mac App Store edition is not yet on sale.
