3. What the product can and cannot do

WYS assists with transcription, speaker attribution, editing, subtitles, summaries and translation. These are fallible automated drafts, not a certified transcript, evidence authentication, professional advice or an emergency/safety system. Even clear audio can produce omissions, invented words, wrong numbers, punctuation, names, dates, negations, speakers or timing. Summaries, translations and suggested corrections can distort meaning; a cleaner sentence is not proof of accuracy.

Quality varies with the recording (noise, echo, clipping, compression, volume, microphones and overlapping voices), language/accent, selected model, settings and available hardware/system features. No accuracy percentage or fitness for a particular recording is guaranteed. Verify important passages against the original audio, including names, amounts, dates, negations and who said what. Keep the original recording. Obtain qualified independent review before medical, legal, financial, employment, safety or other high-impact decisions; never use an unchecked output as the sole basis.
